Training parameters

1. Sign in with Google

Sign-in needs your client_secret.json (Google Cloud OAuth "Desktop app" client):

Images, labels and models are stored in your Drive under My Drive/YoloStudio. A browser window opens for the Gmail sign-in.

Or: no Google Cloud setup (Drive for Desktop)

Install Google Drive for Desktop, sign in with your Gmail, then enter your synced My Drive path, e.g. G:\My Drive.

In this mode: download the worker notebook (or save it into your Drive), open it in Colab and Run all.

2. Open the training worker in Colab (no copy/paste)

Saved to your Drive and opened directly in Colab. There: Runtime > Change runtime type > GPU, then Runtime > Run all, and allow Drive access. Leave it running.
